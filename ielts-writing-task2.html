IELTS Writing Task 2: insho yozish strategiyasi

40 daqiqa, kamida 250 so'z. Bu — akademik modulning eng ko'p ball beradigan qismi (Writingning 2/3 qismi). Ushbu sahifa mezonlardan boshlab, tayyor struktura andozalarigacha bo'lgan amaliy yo'l-yo'rig'ni o'z ichiga oladi.

AI tomonidan yaratilgan material. Bu sahifa umumiy IELTS metodologiyasi asosida Tayanch AI tomonidan tayyorlangan va sohaviy (IELTS eksperti) tekshiruvidan o'tishi kerak. Quyidagi bo'limlarda aniq namunalar yetishmayotgan joylar TODO belgisi bilan ko'rsatilgan.

Baholash mezonlari (4 ta, har biri 25%)

Examiner har bir inshoni to'rtta alohida mezon bo'yicha baholaydi. Ularning farqini bilish — tayyorgarlikning asosi.

Savolga to'liq, to'g'ri va muvozanatli javob berganmisiz? Fikringizni ( agar talab qilinsa) aniq bildirishingiz, dalillaringiz mavzuga tegishli va yetarli chuqurlikda bo'lishi kerak.

Band 7+: fikr aniq, bosqichma-bosqich rivojlanadi, qarama-qarshiliklar ko'rib chiqiladi. Band 5: savolning faqat bir qismiga javob, dalillar zaif.

Abzatslar mantiqiy tartibda, har bir abzatsda bitta markaziy g'oya. Bog'lovchi so'zlar (however, furthermore, therefore) o'rinli ishlatilishi kerak — ko'p emas, to'g'ri.

Xato: har bir jumlaga "Firstly, Secondly, Moreover" qo'shish inshoni sun'iy qiladi. Bog'lovchilar mazmun talab qilganda ishlatilsin.

Mavzuga mos collocation (so'z birikmalari) va sinonimlar ishlatish. "give a solution" o'rniga "propose a viable solution"; "big problem" o'rniga "pressing issue".

Spelling xatolari (ayniqsa Britan/Amerika birligi saqlanmagan holda) Band 6 dan pastga tushiradi.

Turli qurilmalar: complex sentences, conditionals, passive, relative clauses. Muhim: aniqlik xilma-xillikdan muhimroq. 3 ta murakkab jumla va 7 ta xato ≈ Band 6; 6 xil qurilma va 2 xato ≈ Band 7.5.

Asosiy insho turlari

Savol turi javobingizning tuzilishini belgilaydi. Avval savol turini aniqlang, keyin andozani tanlang.

Opinion (To what extent...)

"To what extent do you agree or disagree?" — Fikringizni aniq bildiring (fully / partially) va uni butun insho davomida himoya qiling.

Discussion

"Discuss both views and give your opinion." — Ikkala nuqtai nazarga ham murojaat qiling, lekin oxirida o'z fikringizni ayting.

Advantage / Disadvantage

"Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?" — Afzallik va kamchiliklarni solishtiring va xulosa chiqaring.

Problem / Solution

"What are the causes and what measures can be taken?" — Muammo sabablari + amaldagi yechimlar.

Two-part question

Ikki alohida savol (masalan, sabab + ta'sir). Har ikkitasiga alohida abzats ajrating.

Tayyor struktura andozasi (4 abzats)

Aksariyat Task 2 insholari 4 abzatsdan iborat: Introduction → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion. Vaqtni tejang.

1

Introduction (2–3 jumla)

Background + Thesis. Mavzuni o'z so'zlaringizda qayta yozing (paraphrase), so'ng asosiy fikringizni ayting. Formula: "While [nuqta A] is common, I believe [fikr] because [sabab]."

2

Body Paragraph 1 (TOPIC SENTENCE → explanation → example)

Har bir abzats bitta g'oya. Bosh jumla (topic sentence) g'oyani e'lon qiladi, keyin tushuntirish va aniq misol.

3

Body Paragraph 2 (qarama-qarshilik yoki ikkinchi dalil)

Ikkinchi nuqtai nazarni keltiring va uni himoya qiling. "On the other hand / Nevertheless" orqali bog'lang.

4

Conclusion (1–2 jumla)

Yangi fikr kiritmang. Thesis'ni boshqa so'zlar bilan takrorlang va xulosa chiqaring.

O'zingizni tekshiring
Conclusion qismida qaysi narsa qilinmasligi kerak?

Akademik lug'at va bog'lovchilar

So'z boyligi — repetition'dan qochish va mavzuga mos collocation ishlatish. Quyida tez-tez ishlatiladiganlar.

  • Qo'shimcha: furthermore, moreover, in addition
  • Qarama-qarshilik: however, nevertheless, whereas, by contrast
  • Sabab/natija: therefore, consequently, as a result, due to
  • Misol: for instance, for example, to illustrate

Environment: mitigate climate change, carbon footprint, sustainable development

Technology: digital divide, automate routine tasks, unprecedented access

Education: foster critical thinking, narrow the gap, lifelong learning

"help" → facilitate, contribute to; "increase" → escalate, surge; "think" → argue, maintain, contend; "show" → demonstrate, illustrate.

Tez-tez uchraydigan xatolar

Bu xatolar ko'pincha "bilmay qolingan" tufayli emas, balki vaqt tugashi va rej asizlik tufayli yuzaga keladi.

Memorized shablonlar (template) ga suyanib qolish

Oldindan yodlangan "bo'sh" jumlalar (It is universally accepted that...) examiner tomonidan darhol payqaladi va past baho beradi.

250 so'z chegarasidan pastga tushish

Kamida 250 so'z — aks holda avtomatik penalty. Lekin 300+ ham shart emas; sifat muhim. Vaqtni 40 daqiqaga rejalashtiring.

—

Grammar xatolari sonini ko'paytirib yuborish

Murakkab qurilma uchun shoshilish xato keltiradi. 2-3 ta aniq complex sentence yetarli; xatosiz sodda jumla noto'g'ri murakkabdan yaxshiroq.

—

Inconsistency (Britan/Amerika)

"organise" va "organize" ni aralashtirmang; bitta uslubni tanlang va saqlang.

BAND 6

Many people move to cities because they want better life. Cities have more jobs and good schools. But cities also have problems like traffic and pollution. This is a big issue for governments.

BAND 8

A growing number of individuals relocate to urban centres in pursuit of enhanced employment prospects and superior education. While this rural-to-urban migration stimulates economic activity, it simultaneously places considerable strain on infrastructure, exacerbating traffic congestion and environmental degradation.
