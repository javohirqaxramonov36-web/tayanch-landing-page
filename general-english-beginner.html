Grammar Breakdown: Form, Meaning & Pronunciation (FMP)

Oxford Navigate metodologiyasida har bir grammatik mavzu 3 ta ustun bo'yicha tahlil qilinadi:

1. FORM (Struktura)

Present Simple vs Continuous:

• Subject + Verb(s/es)

• Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing

2. MEANING (Ma'no & Kontekst)

• Present Simple: Doimiy odatlar va ilmiy haqiqatlar.

• Present Continuous: Aynan hozir sodir bo'layotgan harakatlar.

3. PRONUNCIATION (Talaffuz)

• Contractions: I'm / He's / They're

• Endings: /s/, /z/, /ɪz/ (Works, Plays, Watches)
