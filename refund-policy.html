Pul qaytarish siyosati

Tayanch platformasidagi to'lovlar, ixtiyoriy qo'llab-quvvatlash va qaytarish holatlari.

|

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil. Kuchga kirish sanasi: 2026-yil.

1. Umumiy holat

Tayanch'dagi barcha asosiy o'quv materiallari, testlar va vositalar bepul taqdim etiladi. Hozircha pullik kurslar, obuna yoki majburiy to'lovlar mavjud emas. Shuning uchun aksariyat foydalanuvchi uchun "pul qaytarish" holati yuzaga kelmaydi.

2. Ixtiyoriy qo'llab-quvvatlash (ehson)

Saytda ixtiyoriy qo'llab-quvvatlash sahifasi mavjud bo'lishi mumkin. Bu — xarid emas, balki platformani rivojlantirishga qaratilgan ixtiyoriy homiylik. Shuning uchun ehsonlar odatda qaytarilmaydi.

Istisno: agar texnik xato tufayli bir xil summa ikki marta yoki noto'g'ri o'tkazma amalga oshirilgan bo'lsa, xatolar aniqlanganidan keyin 14 kun ichida murojaat qiling — ortiqcha o'tkazma ko'rib chiqiladi va qaytarilishi mumkin.

3. To'lov usullari va joriy holat

Hozirda Payme va Click merchant hisoblari ulanmagan. Shuning uchun ixtiyoriy qo'llab-quvvatlash ehsonlari hozircha qabul qilinmaydi (tugma o'chirilgan holatda). To'lov tizimi ulanmaguncha hech qanday pul o'tkazmasidan foydalanuvchi hisobidan yechilmaydi.

4. Qaytarish so'rovi

Agar sizda qaytarish bo'yicha asosli so'rov bo'lsa (masalan, noto'g'ri ikki marta o'tkazma), quyidagi orqali murojaat qiling: Telegram @tayanch_go. So'rovda o'tkazma sanasi va summasini ko'rsating.

5. Siyosat o'zgarishlari

Kelajakda pullik xizmatlar paydo bo'lganda, ushbu siyosat yangilanadi va yangi to'lov/shartlar alohida e'lon qilinadi.

6. Aloqa

Savollar uchun: Telegram @tayanch_go.

Last updated: 2026. Effective: 2026.

1. General position

All core learning materials, tests and tools on Tayanch are provided free of charge. There are currently no paid courses, subscriptions or mandatory fees. Therefore, for most users a "refund" situation does not arise.

2. Voluntary support (donation)

The site may offer an optional support page. This is not a purchase but a voluntary contribution to develop the platform. Donations are therefore generally non-refundable.

Exception: if a technical error causes a duplicate or erroneous charge, contact us within 14 days of the error being identified — the extra charge will be reviewed and may be refunded.

3. Payment methods and current status

Payme and Click merchant accounts are not connected at this time. Therefore voluntary support donations are not currently processed (the button is disabled). Until a payment system is connected, no money is deducted from any user.

4. Refund request

If you have a valid refund request (e.g. an erroneous duplicate charge), contact us via: Telegram @tayanch_go. Please include the charge date and amount.

5. Changes to this policy

If paid services are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and the new payment terms announced separately.

6. Contact

For questions: Telegram @tayanch_go.
